How this was built
  • Source: hwahae.com/en rankings. Global trending (top 20) plus the top 20 of every list under Skincare, Cleansers, Masks, Suncare, Body, Hair, Baby and Mom, Men and Beauty Accessory.
  • Ingredient and format tags come from keywords in English product names, so they are directional. A product in several lists counts once.
  • Category ranks on Hwahae refresh about weekly, so most category moves are zero between updates. Trending moves faster.
  • Stage calls, theme write ups, the mapping to our categories and the moves suggested are analysis on top of the rankings, not Hwahae data.
  • Review tags are Hwahae's Korean review topics, translated.
